Famous Places

Famous

Registan Square

Samarkand Center

4.9

The most famous historical complex in Central Asia...

Registan Square — the heart of Samarkand and the most famous historical complex in Central Asia. The word "Registan" comes from Persian, meaning "sandy place".

Registan Square is surrounded by three magnificent madrasas:

  • Ulugbek Madrasa (15th century)
  • Sherdor Madrasa (17th century)
  • Tillaqori Madrasa — decorated with gold leaf

Registan is included in the UNESCO World Heritage list.

14th–15th c. 08:00–20:00 20,000 UZS
Religious

Bibi Khanum Mosque

Northern Samarkand

5.0

A magnificent mosque built by Amir Timur...

Bibi Khanum Mosque is one of the greatest historical monuments in Samarkand. It was built in the 14th–15th centuries by Amir Timur.

The mosque features a magnificent portal, high domes, and a spacious courtyard with unique architectural solutions.

Thousands of tourists visit this site every year.

14th–15th c. 07:00–19:00 15,000 UZS
Sacred

Shahi Zinda

Northeast Samarkand

4.8

A sacred complex of mausoleums...

Shahi Zinda means "Living King". It is believed that Qusam ibn Abbas, cousin of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), is buried here.

The complex was formed between the 11th–15th centuries and consists of many mausoleums, mosques, and other architectural structures.

The stairway path of the complex is very famous.

11th–15th c. 08:00–18:00 12,000 UZS
Scientific

Ulugbek Observatory

Southern Samarkand

5.0

The largest astronomical center of the 15th century...

Ulugbek Observatory was built in the 15th century by the great scientist and ruler Mirzo Ulugbek.

The main part is a sextant with a radius of 40 meters (used for measuring stars).

Scientists like Qazi Zada Rumi and Ali Qushchi worked here.

15th century 09:00–17:00 10,000 UZS
Historical

Afrosiyob Museum

Northern Samarkand

4.7

Ruins of the ancient city of Afrosiyob...

Afrosiyob Museum is one of the oldest historical sites in Samarkand, containing archaeological remains from the 7th century BC and later periods.

The museum collection includes:

  • Ancient frescoes
  • Ceramic products
  • Ancient weapons

Amir Timur

Gur Emir Mausoleum

Samarkand Center

5.0

Mausoleum of Amir Timur and the Timurid dynasty...

Gur Emir is the mausoleum of Amir Timur and members of the Timurid dynasty, built in the 15th century.

Buried here are Amir Timur, Shahrukh, Mironshah, Ulugbek, and Muhammad Sultan.

Gur Emir served as architectural inspiration for the Taj Mahal.

15th century 08:00–19:00 18,000 UZS
